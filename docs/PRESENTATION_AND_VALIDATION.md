# Presentation and validation gate

Recorded 2 October 2026 before submission. The product is a prototype; no external active users, paid customers or completed public migration are established.

## Central demonstration

Lead with the recipient-split regression: total migration fee 15%, intended creator share 100%, decoded creator share 50%. The [local-program artifact](proof/localnet-production-flow.json) records a real config transaction and this single comparison mismatch. It is local proof, not a public mainnet transaction or evidence of an actual user's loss.

Then show the independent existing mainnet configuration through the production reader. These are two distinct evidence paths. The initial demo now loads a default unsigned draft and highlights differences before the full receipt. A share link carries the live account, not the draft.

## Demand experiment

Target launchpad engineers who actually review DBC configuration economics. Ask how they verify recipient shares today, whether a comparison changes a decision, which fields are missing and whether they would repeat a review. Preserve raw responses, dates, relevant role, exact configuration tested and permission for attribution. Count completed reviews separately from invitations or impressions.

Do not invent a positive finding. A negative outcome means reconsidering the thesis. No spending on a mainnet launch solely for appearance. No messages or public posts are authorized by this file.

2. No own mainnet launch, completed migration, partner integration or external usage proves impact.
3. A technically working prototype can lose through incomplete evidence or weak presentation. Link every required outcome to proof; implemented is not executed.

These are assessed risks, not actual judge reasons or a win percentage. Preserve this record after results and append new facts with dates.
