Developer tooling · Meteora DBC

Check the economics before linking a launch.

CurveReceipt compares a builder's selected draft terms with a live Meteora Dynamic Bonding Curve configuration or pool. No wallet connection is needed for inspection.

Prototype pitch · 2 October 2026 · AI-assisted implementation

01 · Intended customer and problem

A headline fee is an incomplete review.

A launchpad builder needs to check fee timing, graduation recipients and liquidity allocations against the economics they intended. Two configurations can advertise the same total graduation fee while paying different creator shares.

This problem is a product hypothesis. We have not verified customer interviews, demand or willingness to pay.

02 · Working product

Model → inspect → compare → share.

  1. Model a fixed-supply SOL-quoted launch using Meteora's official SDK curve builder.
  2. Read an arbitrary config or pool, check DBC program ownership and RPC network, then decode readable economics.
  3. Compare 24 selected terms with an editable local draft, including fee recipients and LP allocations.
  4. Share the live account URL. The receiving tab rereads the account; the local draft is absent.

The draft is unsigned. A matching selected-term comparison does not authenticate a creator promise, verify the full curve or establish token safety.

04 · Differentiation

A narrow check for existing builder workflows.

Meteora Invent and launchpad scaffolds already create launches; meteora.fyi already explains curated configurations. CurveReceipt focuses on comparing a saved draft with an arbitrary live account. We do not claim to be the first receipt tool or a full economic audit.

The comparison must earn a place in a real builder's workflow. A standalone prototype does not yet demonstrate that value.

05 · Proposed distribution and business test

Validate the workflow before adding a business model.

Proposed first test: invite five launchpad builders to review an actual configuration against their intended terms, record whether the comparison catches a useful difference and whether they return for a second review. No interviews or outreach are claimed as completed.

Distribution hypothesis: an optional receipt link in launchpad review screens and SDK examples. Any embed or partner integration is future work.

Potential paid use: team review history and approval workflows, only if repeat usage and willingness to pay are validated. The current inspection prototype is free; there is no validated price, market-size estimate or revenue forecast.
